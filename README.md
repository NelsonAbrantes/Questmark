# Questmark

An objective tracker, a dungeon card and a quest book for WoW: Forever. The quest book opens on the quest log key.

**Status: beta.** Made for WoW: Forever only.

## Features

- **Its own tracker.** Quests by zone, achievements and recipes, with their objectives, quest items and progress.
- **Dungeon card.** In dungeons: the run's time, the bosses with their faces (killed ones marked), deaths, and the experience and gold made.
- **Quest book.** The quest log key (L) opens a book: the quests by zone on the left page, in their difficulty colors, and on the right the quest chosen, with its objectives, its description, its rewards and buttons to show it on its map, track, share or abandon it. It opens and shuts as a book and turns its pages, stamps "Completed" on quests done and turns abandoned ones to dust; it can be resized from its page corner.
- **Options** in the game's Options window (Options > AddOns > Questmark, with a page of its own for the quest book), or `/qm`. The tracker and the quest book can each be turned off.
- **Edit Mode.** Move it and change its main settings in the game's Edit Mode.
