# Questmark

An objective tracker, a dungeon card and a quest book for WoW: Forever. The quest book opens on the quest log key.

**Status: beta.** Made for WoW: Forever only.

## Features

- **Its own tracker.** Quests by zone and recipes, with their objectives, quest items and progress.
- **Dungeon card.** In dungeons: the run's time, the bosses with their faces (killed ones marked), deaths, and the experience and gold made.
- **Quest book.** The quest log key (L) opens a book: the quests by zone on the left page, in their difficulty colors, and on the right the quest chosen, with its objectives, its description, its rewards and buttons to show it on its map, track, share or abandon it. It opens and shuts as a book and turns its pages, stamps "Completed" on quests done and turns abandoned ones to dust; it can be resized from its page corner. Its red ribbon lists the quests you turned in, by day, each with its text and rewards.
- **Quest GPS.** Follow a quest (click its icon in the tracker): an arrow on the screen points the way, or the game's navigation marker shows over the place.
- **Works with** Questie, ForeverDungeonJournal and AtlasLoot (click a boss in the dungeon card for its loot).
- **Options** in the game's Options window (Options > AddOns > Questmark, with pages of their own for the quest book and the GPS), or `/qm`. The tracker and the quest book can each be turned off.
- **Edit Mode.** Move it and change its main settings in the game's Edit Mode.
