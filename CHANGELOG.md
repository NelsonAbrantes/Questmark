# Changelog

Versions are named `X.Y.Z`: `0.x` while in beta.

## 0.3.2 (beta)

- Share is back: in combat a message instead of an error

## 0.3.1 (beta)

- Fix: the navigation marker shows again
- Fix: Stormwind Stockade bosses on the dungeon card
- Share taken out: the game blocks it from addons
- Bigger search clear button

## 0.3.0 (beta)

- Quest book: completed quests behind a red ribbon, by day
- Completed quests show their text and rewards, the one chosen in color
- Turn-in XP in the Quests title
- Dungeon records
- Follows the next quest after a turn-in
- Job's done for the Alliance
- Navigation marker: Class icon style
- Fix: dungeon bosses made of several creatures are checked off only when all of them die

## 0.2.3 (beta)

- Navigation marker: Quest mark, Faction crest and Sword styles
- Navigation marker fades near the objective and hides in dungeons
- Quest book: Page brightness option
- Reward tooltips follow the mouse
- Bigger zone names in the tracker
- Fixes: marker blinking or showing with nothing followed, uneven zone lines

## 0.2.2 (beta)

- Quest book search (the magnifying glass by the title)
- The book opens with no quest chosen
- Ctrl-click a reward to try it on, Shift-click to link it
- Quest icons in the tracker light up under the mouse
- The book sits with the game's windows

## 0.2.1 (beta)

- Brushed paint tracker background (option)
- Navigation marker shapes, size and color
- Dungeon mark on dungeon zones in the tracker
- Following a quest opens its zone in the tracker
- Asks which tracker to keep when Questie's is on
- Dungeon card XP and gold no longer count quests
- Objective tooltip on the book's map
- Fixes: book over other windows, Share and Show map buttons, map tape, abandon animation

## 0.2.0 (beta)

- Quest GPS arrow and navigation marker
- Click a quest name to open it in the book, its icon to follow it
- Questie support
- Click a boss face for its loot (needs ForeverDungeonJournal or AtlasLoot)
- Dungeon card resets with the dungeon
- Boss faces shared with the group
- Auto accept and turn in quests (option)
- Quest levels (option)
- Quest book list font, size and colors
- Shift-click a quest in the book to link it

## 0.1.0 (beta)

First release.

- Its own objective tracker: quests by zone and recipes, with quest items and progress.
- Dungeon card: run time, bosses, deaths, experience and gold.
- Quest book on the quest log key: quest list, quest page, map, rewards, Track, Share and Abandon.
- Book animations: opening cover, page turns, a "Completed" stamp, abandoned quests turning to dust.
- Options (Options > AddOns > Questmark, or `/qm`) and Edit Mode settings.
